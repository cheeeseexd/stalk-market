## Frozen rule and implementation

- Eligibility uses the immediately preceding index session, with all six hourly bars present; sum(volume × hourly close) ≥ ₹10 crore and last close ≥ ₹50. This is a traded-value proxy; missing/zero opening volumes are not imputed.
- Exclude results dates ±3 calendar days from cached purpose/detail text, H17 price-effect ex-dates including dividends, H15 flags, and >1% hourly-open or preceding-close disagreement with cached official OPEN/PREVCLOSE. Missing official symbol references are excluded as unknown. H17 dated and API-symbol aliases are both excluded conservatively. Calendar and action exclusions precede industry medians and ranking.
- At 11:15, first-hour return is 09:15-bar close/open−1; subtract the eligible same-industry median, self included. Relative ≤−2% plus second-hour close/open−1 ≥+0.5% selects long; mirrored conditions select short. No minimum peer count was invented; sparse groups remain visible in power.json. NIFTY is backdrop, not a panic prerequisite.
- Rank absolute relative return descending, then symbol; maximum two total positions. Reference entry is 11:15 open. Each slot is ₹5,000, integer shares, no leverage or replacement of an unaffordable pick. Stops are 1.5% and prescribed diagnostic 2.5% from slipped entry. Use 11:15–13:15 ranges; gap through stop fills at worse open. Exit at 14:15 open, including an adverse stop gap at that open. 14:15 high/low/close cannot affect a result.
- Variant a removes only reversal. Variant b retains reversal and requires the trade to agree with NIFTY’s 09:15 open to 10:15-bar close direction; zero/missing direction permits no market-filtered trade. Filters apply before ranking.
- Adverse slippage per side: Next50 4, Midcap 6, Smallcap 10 bps. Side-aware Zerodha MIS charges use the registered H14 schedule: 0.03% brokerage capped ₹20/order, sell STT 0.025%, buy stamp 0.003%, NSE 0.00307%, SEBI 0.0001%, IPFT and applicable 18% GST. Schedule is frozen across history; it was not freshly verified online and engine costs were not changed.

## Integrity, limitations and timing check

Official day files exist for all 726 sessions, but symbol references are incomplete. Of 174,240 wider symbol-days, 15,741 lack a usable official opening/reference pair, 19,408 have >1% hourly/reference disagreement, 13,557 match a results/action/H15 exclusion, 147,408 meet liquidity/price requirements, and 120,036 survive the joint eligibility screen. Counts overlap and are not additive. Two index sessions lack a usable two-hour direction. The mismatch extension and missing-reference exclusion were registered before returns, not chosen to improve P&L.

Results ±3 is a retrospective cache exclusion, not certified point-in-time knowledge; unknown notice publication timestamps use H17’s advance-ex-date assumption. Current symbols/industries and API aliases are conservative identity proxies. Raw official prices need not be action-neutral. The shared current fee schedule and tier slippage are assumptions, not historical measured execution evidence. Hourly stop ranges cannot prove executable fills, circuit access or short MIS eligibility. Portfolio ranking at 11:15 and filling at that same timestamp still requires a delay/quote check before any deployment.

The 5-minute set spans 2026-08-05–2026-10-30, entirely inside reserved confirmation. There is zero design overlap, so no 5-minute post-entry timing/stop outcome was inspected. Its diagnostic is explicitly unavailable under the closed gate; it was not relabelled as confirmation.

## One lesson and next daily idea

**Lesson: enough daily opportunities do not establish an edge; waiting for a reversal did not clear the cost hurdle.** Neither dropping reversal nor imposing market agreement is a new primary result.

Next daily idea (H19, count/specify first): a failed break of the prior-day high/low after 10:15, followed by re-entry into yesterday’s range. Long after reclaiming the prior low; short after rejecting the prior high, with causal next-five-minute entries and the same capital/execution discipline. Hypothesis: trapped breakout flow, not the size of an industry-relative first-hour move. Count independent days, both sides and achievable fills before any returns; these reused dates can supply power only, and validation needs fresh prospective observations. Do not tune H18 or promise this will trade profitably every day. H16 quote/MIS/circuit evidence remains the prerequisite and queue leader.
