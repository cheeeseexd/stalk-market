## Price integrity: material failure, no post-hoc rescue

`price_scale_audit.csv/json` compares the **same session's** signal-time intraday open with the official daily open; it never uses same-day close/return. TDPOWERSYS on Aug 5: intraday 569.75, official 1,142.00, preceding official close 1,127.50. That constructs -49.47% from two price scales while the official opening gap is about +1.29%. Seven selected TDPOWERSYS mornings have intraday/official-open ratios around 0.5. The eighth is Aug 24: intraday/official opens agree near 775, but the predecessor official close is still 1,534.80. A cached Aug 12 NSE Record Date announcement explicitly identifies Aug 24 as a split/subdivision record date (	dpowersys_announcements.json and the hashed source in the price audit). Thus the eight apparent large falls include seven source-scale mismatches and a known corporate-action date. Attribution of the earlier intraday rescaling to that action remains an inference; the adjustment formula was not reconstructed. A nonflat first bar does not repair a false gap.

The diagnostic >1% opening-price-difference flag fires for 11/25 design and 4/14 hold-out picks, but only seven design TDPOWERSYS picks show the near-2:1 open issue (the eighth is the separately identified split-record-date gap). Smaller discrepancies can arise from different opening prints and do not establish adjustment errors. Hold-out comparison used signal-time opens only, with zero near-2:1 flags; it did not read paths or score returns. No clean substitute portfolio, alternate close source, symbol exclusion, price rescaling or rerun was constructed after observing design outcomes. `result.json` retains the original machine gate result DESIGN_REJECTED; `final_review.json` adds the data-integrity verdict so the arithmetic output is not mistaken for clean evidence.

Other limits: current surviving-universe membership/tiers, H9's inherited 09:08 news cutoff, retrospectively retrieved announcement/event content and possible calendar revisions, unresolved corporate actions/price units, unknown historical MIS acceptance and short restrictions, missing bid/ask/depth/circuit limits. The first-bar lock proxy alone cannot establish an executable quote. Across ordinary days the feed reports 13,417 zero opening-volume stock-days despite nonflat candles; this is audited rather than treated as circuit evidence. A 09:20 bar open plus assumed slippage is a more timely proxy than official-open entry, still not a measured fill. Restoring price units also matters for historical whole-share sizing; simply changing the gap denominator is insufficient proof of executable entries.
