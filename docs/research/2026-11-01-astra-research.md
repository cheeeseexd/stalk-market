# Independent Indian trading research — 1 November 2026

The strongest next step is validating executable panic-buy returns, followed by a tightly controlled H31 experiment. I found evidence of Indian price pressure and reversal, but no independently established, modern, cost-positive daily cash-equity strategy matching this account. Existing project results below are supplied findings, not independently replicated. No backtests or trading were performed.

**Costs change the ranking.** At ₹5,000 notional, current [Zerodha charges](https://zerodha.com/charges) imply approximately 0.106% intraday round trip, but **0.529% delivery/BTST**, including ₹15.34 DP, before spread/slippage. [BTST incurs DP charges](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/dp-charges-for-btst-trades) and requires CNC. Thus 0.107% average overnight index drift cannot fund daily stock BTST. H31’s reported 0.08% net is ₹4/trade, approximately ₹1,200 over 300 trades; another 10 basis points of execution friction makes it negative.

**What the sources establish**

- **Overnight/opening effects:** [Zargar–Kumar, 2019](https://file.scirp.org/pdf/TEL_2019010916410835.pdf), studying Nifty constituents over January 2000–May 2016, reports 2,988 observations per return series, mean overnight +0.20% and daytime −0.14%. It supports the decomposition, not executable profits: missing prices are imputed using five-day averages, its noise decomposition assumes a noise-free close, and its proposed gap fade has no costed trading validation.
- **Intraday evidence:** [Patnaik–Thomas](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=568363) uses NSE order books, 1996–2002, calculating executable prices for ₹10,000 orders; some momentum profits survive impact and bid–ask corrections. This is unusually relevant methodology but ancient market structure. [Tripathi–Aggarwal](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2608317) reports reversal within minutes after extreme moves; the accessible abstract supplies neither sample size nor net returns. Neither validates H31 specifically.
- **Modern practitioner research:** [Alpha Alternatives’ 2024 study](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Aanjaneya-Sharma/publication/383567351_Hedging_Demand_and_Intraday_Momentum_within_the_Indian_Stock_Market/links/66deae70b1606e24c21b276c/Hedging-Demand-and-Intraday-Momentum-within-the-Indian-Stock-Market.pdf) selects 44 liquid stock futures from 273, using minute data January 2021–July 2024. Its practical late-day model reports Sharpe 1.43, explicitly **excluding transaction costs** and after extensive window optimization. Interesting mechanism; not a retail net-edge demonstration.
- **Rebalancing:** [NSE/IIM Kozhikode research](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/research/comppaper90.pdf), 1996–2003, finds +1.47% effective-day abnormal return for additions, reversing by day nine; only 33 effective-day addition observations after clustering adjustments. A [2010–2024 study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5642110), 81 usable events, finds weaker/asymmetric reactions. [MSCI research](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/4476422.pdf?abstractid=4476422&mirid=1&type=2), 56 markets over 2006–2023, supports predictable implementation-day pressure, but is not an India-only estimate. FTSE needs separate validation.
- **Delivery/bulk/block deals:** [Amrita’s study](https://webfiles.amrita.edu/2021/11/testing-weak-form-efficiency-in-banking-sector-of-indian-stock-market-by-examing-the-relationship-between-delivery-quantity-to-total-quantity-traded-and-returns-of-stocks.pdf), 13 banks, February 2015–February 2017, finds no delivery-percentage-to-return Granger causality. [Gupta’s 125 BSE block deals](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2691376), 2006–2012, average +1.32% on the event day; [bulk-deal research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S0307435822000842), 2010–2019, emphasizes pre-event gains. These do not establish profits after public disclosure. Same-day final delivery percentage is unavailable at 09:30.
- **ASM/circuits/F&O:** [NSE-sponsored ASM research](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/s3fs-public/inline-files/Effectiveness%20of%20additional%20surveillance%20measures_WorkingPaper.pdf), 218 events/188 firms in 2018–2019, finds stabilization and reduced liquidity, not a costed strategy. [Zerodha restrictions](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/alerts-and-nudges/nudges/articles/surveillance-indicators) make T2T and surveillance status essential eligibility data. Circuit-locked stops are not fills. I found no convincing net-return proof that F&O membership alone predicts intraday direction; test dated F&O/non-F&O groups matched on liquidity and size.
- **Expiry/sector rotation:** A [2013–2019 expiry study](https://www.econjournals.com/index.php/ijefi/article/download/9429/pdf/23321), with 37 expiries in each principal subsample, finds significant volume differences but insignificant mean-return differences. Your intraday result is more directly relevant, but expiry dates changed to [Tuesdays in September 2025](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP68747.pdf). [Liquidity-conditioned Indian momentum](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pacfin.2023.102193), 3,956 stocks/2000–2021, concerns longer horizons; it does not establish 1–3-day sector rotation.
- **Community/video checks:** An [IndianStreetBets practitioner](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndianStreetBets/comments/nkva3n) reports a ten-year backtest but up to 1.5% discrepancy between recorded open and tradable 09:15 prices: useful execution evidence, not verified alpha. [Stockmock’s video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3I6MAOYEApM) explains BTST backtesting, not demonstrated performance. Another [five-year-backtest promotion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hr4ucNXp42U) was private on direct retrieval. No accessible transcript/trade ledger substantiated it; no reproducible numerical cash-equity edge surfaced from IndiaInvestments searches.

**Diagnosis and H31 controls**

Final auction gaps cannot simultaneously select winners and guarantee fills at those auction prices. Daily official closes also need not equal executable last prices. [NSE’s current pre-open rules](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-pre-open) now randomize collection closure between 09:08–09:10; retain historical rules and separate the [2026 closing-auction regime](https://www.nseindia.com/static/reports/closing-auction-session-historical-data).

Audit point-in-time universes, corporate actions, news timestamps, broker eligibility, and stop gap-throughs. Verify Kite bar timestamp semantics: a bar beginning 09:30 is not known at 09:30. Cluster uncertainty by date; 285 stocks are not 285 independent market experiments. Compare H31 against matched same-time shorts to remove ordinary downward drift. Test ≥5% and liquidity/F&O restrictions separately before combining them. A “first trade profitable” rule may use only profit observable when the second entry would occur. Freeze rules before inspecting arriving 2015–2020 data; that is useful backward falsification, not prospective validation. More filters cannot repair a weak economic margin.

**Ranked experiments**

Ranking is qualitative expected net-edge plausibility × data testability; these exact rules are proposals, not published profitable replications. Use ₹5,000 integer-share slots, maximum two concurrent positions, and account for tied-up cash. Stress intraday costs at 0.206% and 0.306% total; delivery at ≥0.63%.

1. **Executable panic buy — highest priority.** Freeze existing NIFTY-open <−0.5%, two-largest no-news small/midcap gap-down selections; enter at 09:20 bar open, 3% stop, exit 15:10. Mechanism: liquidity provision to indiscriminate panic sellers; source: supplied evidence and [opening-noise research](https://file.scirp.org/pdf/TEL_2019010916410835.pdf). Bars exist; historical news/eligibility timestamps need verification. If delayed fills destroy returns, retire the claimed executable edge.
2. **Liquid extreme H31 — second.** At 09:30 require ≥5% above previous close; cumulative 09:15–09:30 volume ≥10× its preceding-20-session same-window median; 09:25–09:30 close in lower half. Restrict to dated F&O names in the upper half of lagged 60-day median turnover. Short the two largest rises at 09:35; 3% stop; exit 15:10. Counterparty: attention-driven buyers, consistent with [extreme-move reversal](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2608317). Bars exist; dated F&O/eligibility lists are missing from the stated inventory.
3. **Implementation-session additions — third.** On the final session before an announced index change becomes effective, buy up to two additions with highest prior-20-day median turnover at 09:20; 3% stop; exit 15:10. Counterparty: benchmark-tracking funds buying toward implementation. [MSCI mechanism](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/4476422.pdf?abstractid=4476422&mirid=1&type=2). Prices exist; announcement/effective-date tables must be collected separately for Nifty, MSCI and FTSE. Exiting before closing flows may eliminate the edge; measure this explicitly.
4. **Conditional BTST reversal — lowest confidence.** At 15:05, select two largest open-to-current decliners down ≥3% while the universe median is above −0.5%; buy CNC at 15:10, sell next session 09:20. No assumed overnight stop protection. Mechanism hypothesis: temporary stock-specific selling reverses overnight; [daily reversal research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13245) motivates, but does not validate, this rule. Bars suffice for a price-only screen. That paper’s 2014–2021 results select among 237 daily variants and use long/short portfolios. Reject unless gross returns comfortably clear delivery friction.

**ML ranker**

Worth one bounded experiment, not an indicator search engine. Pool stocks; use shallow gradient boosting on gap, relative opening return, same-window relative volume, range location, volatility and liquidity. Predict executable 09:35–15:10 return; rank short candidates by expected net P&L. Benchmark against H31 and a linear model. Use chronological rolling three-year training, six-month validation, then six-month tests; never random stock-row splits. Fit preprocessing only on training data, purge overlapping labels, and reserve genuinely unseen future data. Evaluate two-slot daily portfolio returns, date-block confidence intervals, tail losses and cost sensitivity—not classification accuracy. [Indian decision-tree research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13959) does not establish this costed ranker’s profitability. Abstention must be allowed.

Panic-buy remains the strongest lead, conditional on executable entries.
H31’s eight-basis-point net margin is too fragile to trust yet.
Index implementation flows deserve a small, timestamped event study.
₹5,000 BTST positions face approximately 0.53% fees before execution friction.
Use ML to rank and abstain; require untouched, costed portfolio evidence.
