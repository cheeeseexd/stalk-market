# Research notes, 1 Nov 2026: what other people say works for Indian intraday, and what we take from it

This is the one place for everything gathered so far. The earlier notes (`2026-10-30.md`) cover the X threads, the Claude skills and the ₹5,000 test.
Every claim here is self-reported unless it says otherwise. None of these sources gives an audited, cost-inclusive track record.

## Hard evidence

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| [SEBI study of intraday traders, FY23 (July 2024)](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/over-70-intra-day-traders-incur-losses-during-fy23-reveals-sebi-study-124072401110_1.html) | 71% of individual intraday traders in the equity cash segment lost money. 80% of those with more than 500 trades a year lost. Loss-makers paid an extra 57% of their losses in charges; profit-makers gave up 19% of their profits to charges. |
| Our own runs (RUNBOOK.md) | On NIFTY 50 stocks with ₹100 risk, charges cost 0.26R a trade at 5-minute stops, 0.13R at wider stops and 0.10R on 15-minute bars. Every breakout and trend rule was slightly negative even before charges. Gap fade is the only rule positive at every setting. |

## What we take from all of it

1. **Rules over predictions.** Every source that did well credits discipline: fixed risk, no stop-moving, no revenge trades, timeouts. See RULES.md.
2. **Hold winners.** The video's average win was 3× its loss because trades were held for days. Our best trades are the ones held to the close.
   Intraday MIS can't hold overnight, so a daily-chart swing version (delivery, no leverage) is the next thing worth testing.
3. **Test long and unseen.** Two years of hourly bars give about 650 sessions the rules were never tuned on. That's the test that matters, not another week.
4. **Count every try.** Choosing the best variant after the fact is curve-fitting (the Groww video's warning). The trials ledger and the plateau rule stay.
