## Lesson and next step

Additional ranks can earn positive amounts yet dilute the Rs10,000 portfolio because every original pick gets less capital. They add correlated panic exposure, not more trading days. No runner is changed. H16 timestamped quotes, MIS/circuit evidence and delayed executable fills remain the next prerequisite.
