## Adopted

- Rules from the theses: `orb_breakout` (Shivansh, agents-trade), `momentum_52w` (Pranjal, 52-Week High Momentum), `vwap_pullback` (skill-algotrader), and `gap_fade` (Ganesh), kept as a falsification test.
- Validation: ≥ 40 trades, t ≥ 1 after charges, and a plateau across neighbouring settings. A trials ledger records every setting tried. A holdout (19–30 Oct) is looked at once.
- Time Machine odds on every setup, and a risk gate that gives every decision a reason.
- Risk rules: ≤ 2 trades a day, 1 position, ₹300 loss halt, stop after 2 losses in a row, 45-minute cooldown, a midday lull, a cost gate, and tick rounding.
- Live safety: SL-M at the broker on entry; an exit converts the stop to MARKET rather than cancelling it; reconcile on startup; a static-IP guard; the 15:20 auto square-off is only a backstop.
- Process: RUNBOOK.md each session, two independent reviewer skills before any real order, and a shadow account beside the desk.

## Rejected (for now)

- **Options buying on ₹10k**: one Nifty ATM lot (65 units × ₹100–200) uses up most of the account, and there's no free intraday option-chain history to backtest it honestly.
- **An LLM in the order path**: Claude researches and reviews. Drex only votes, and its vote is scored, not obeyed.
- **QuantGPT and paid data**: Yahoo (60 days of 5-minute bars, 2 years daily) is free. Kite historical data needs the paid ₹2,000/mo Kite Connect plan.

## ₹10,000 maths

- Zerodha MIS gives about 5× on equity, so up to about ₹50,000 of exposure.
- A ₹50k round trip costs about ₹50–55: brokerage ₹15 + ₹15, STT ₹12.50, exchange, SEBI, stamp and GST.
- In R, charges depend on the stop width, not on position size. At 1.5 × 5-minute ATR they are about **0.26R per trade** on NIFTY 50 stocks (measured).
- Intraday profit is speculative business income, taxed at your slab rate. Tax is a share of profit, so it never turns a profitable day into a loss. Charges can.
