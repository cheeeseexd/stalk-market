# H27b: panic attribution with complete prior features

Executed 1 November 2026. **Passed the registered stock-selection screen:** strict picks beat ordinary same-morning controls by **196.80 basis points**, with **day t = 6.28**. Matching covers **313 of 390 retained picks (80.26%) on 191 independent mornings**, just above the 80% coverage gate. This is a gross historical attribution result, not a new net-profit strategy test or permission to trade.

H27b was registered separately in RUNBOOK before returns. Its exclusive `data/h27b/started.json` permits one scored run; exactly one was completed. H27 remains Unresolved and all its files and guard are unchanged. No settings, dates, controls or thresholds were changed after seeing outcomes.

## The population was fixed before outcomes

H27 tried to explain every frozen H17 pick and stopped because some prior features were unavailable. The user expressly authorized a different question: explain the original picks that have the required prior history. No excluded pick was replaced and the original top-two ranking was checked before applying history eligibility.

“Complete prior features” keeps H27's definitions: 60 preceding index sessions with at least 45 paired stock/index returns for beta; at least 15 returns in the preceding 20 for sample volatility; all 20 preceding official turnover observations for mean turnover. Beta must be finite; volatility and turnover must be finite and positive. It does not mean 60 valid returns out of 60. Controls face the same eligibility rule. Today's close never enters eligibility or matching.

## What was compared

Everything beyond history eligibility follows H27. The control pool uses delisted-inclusive historical NSE EQ bhavcopy and H17's liquidity, price, action, fund and retrospective results exclusions. There is no cached-tier requirement. Opening locks remain H17's inherited unknown/kept assumption.

Each pick needs five distinct same-morning controls. All original selected names are excluded from controls, including history-ineligible selections. Ordinary controls can have any gap greater than −2%. The second set is at least 2% down and below the original top two. Nearest distance uses prior beta, volatility and turnover only, with beta difference at most 0.5, volatility ratio at most 2 and turnover ratio at most 3. Ties use symbol order. Fewer than five is unmatched; no caliper was relaxed. Controls may be reused across picks.

Returns run from official open to official close, without stops or costs. Five controls are averaged per pick, picks per morning, then independent mornings equally. The required screen is at least 80% matching coverage of retained picks, at least 40 matched mornings, excess strictly above 50 bps and day t at least 2.5. The second comparison cannot rescue the primary result.

## Did the whole market rebound?

“Ordinary stocks” below means every history-eligible ordinary stock in the same H17 pool, with stocks averaged within each morning and mornings equally. It is broader than the five matched controls. NIFTY uses its own cached daily open and close; it is not a traded index instrument or certified NSE auction price.

| Loose, all retained active mornings | 336 | −10.59 | +2.62 |
| Loose, ordinary-matched mornings only | 308 | −10.57 | +0.49 |

The picks beat ordinary controls, so **stock-specific overshoot is real in this measured population under the registered comparison**. Ordinary stocks did not rise strongly on average. These results do not support explaining the recovery mainly as a broad market rebound. If picks had failed to beat ordinary controls while ordinary stocks rose strongly, the interpretation would instead be mostly market rebound, and the natural cheap implementation would be an index trade to test later. No index strategy was tested here.

Beating other also-gapped stocks would support the ranking. The observed excess is positive, but that set covers only 42.56% of strict and 28.39% of loose picks. **Ranking remains unresolved for the retained population.**

The history is reused, controls are observational, and unmatched picks remain outside the scored contrast. The strict coverage margin is narrow. Retrospective results calendars, action-identity assumptions, unknown opening locks and assumed official-price execution are inherited. This supports the stock-selection explanation conditionally; it does not establish that the sellers were uninformed or that these opening prices can be filled. No live rule or allocation was changed.

## Monday's pre-open recorder

The existing five-second Agent execution loop now also drives `stalk/research/preopen_log.py`. From Monday 2 November, while Kite is logged in, it records 09:01, 09:04, 09:07, 09:08:30 and the 09:15 open snapshot, all IST. It requests the twelve watchlist names, NIFTY 50, provisional live pre-open gap candidates, and any morning candidates already observed. Earlier observed candidate names are carried through to the open.

The 09:10 brief cannot identify candidates for earlier slots. At auction slots the recorder therefore uses the existing live NSE market adapter to observe provisional candidates in the frozen forward universe with gaps below −2%. This is data collection, not a final trade ranking. Source failures are recorded as unknown while watchlist/index quotes are still attempted. Future brief or runner decisions never reconstruct an earlier candidate set.

Each envelope stores scheduled, request and receipt times, candidate provenance and Kite's returned indicative price if present, last price, buy/sell totals and depth, including returned order counts and level ordering. Missing values are null. Last price is never relabelled as indicative price. Stale or timestamp-unknown quotes remain labelled. The open is quote `ohlc.open`, accepted as the observed opening price only with a timely same-day quote timestamp at or after 09:15; the returned raw open is separately retained. This is not a realised fill or a guarantee that Kite supplies auction-specific imbalance.

Files are `data/execution/preopen_<day>.jsonl`. Completed envelopes are deduplicated across restart. Missed slots stay missed; there is no historical reconstruction. A 60-second observation window accommodates the Agent poll and request latency, with actual times preserved and late responses labelled. When Kite is logged out the pre-open recorder performs no source calls, quote calls or file writes. It never logs in or invokes an order tool.

GET `/api/execution` keeps its existing `execution` value and adds a sibling `preopen` value, null before observations. Existing H16 aggregates and review fields retain their meaning. No web files or Agent trading guards were changed. Start the normal app in paper mode and log into Kite before 09:01 Monday; this task did not start a server or leave a collection process running.
