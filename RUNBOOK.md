**What it means**
- **Charges are a fixed tax of about 0.26R per trade at this stop width.** In R, charges depend on stop width,
  not on position size: cost/R = charge% × price / stop distance. A 1.5 × 5-minute ATR stop is about 0.3% of price,
  and ₹25–55 of charges on a ₹23–50k position is about 0.1%. Raising risk per trade doesn't help (checked: ₹300 gives the same R).
- **The rules are also slightly negative before charges** (gross about −0.11R to −0.13R). So the problem isn't only cost.
- **The account takes the morning's first two signals**, which do worse than the average signal (−0.61R vs −0.38R on ORB).
  Parity was checked trade by trade: the engine reproduces research exactly, so this is selection, not a bug.
- **The Time Machine doesn't predict outcomes here.** Trades it gave 35–50% target odds hit target 12.5% of the time,
  versus 13.5% for the 0–20% bucket. Part of that is by design (it ignores the breakeven move), but it shouldn't rank setups until it calibrates.
- Gap-up fade lost again, as it did for Ganesh live. The 52-week momentum sample is far too small (10 trades in 48 days on NIFTY 50).
- This agrees with the threads: no source showed a validated edge, and Shivansh found none on non-expiry days.

## 2026-11-01 (later): what was wrong with the long test, and a month played day by day
